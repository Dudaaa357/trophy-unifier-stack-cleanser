## Overview 🌐

StackForge is a next-generation trophy aggregation platform that reimagines how collectors manage their digital gaming achievements. Born from the observation that modern trophy hunters juggle multiple regional versions, remasters, and re-releases of the same title, StackForge introduces a revolutionary **Fusion Protocol**—a smart merging system that intelligently consolidates duplicate trophy sets while preserving the integrity of your true accomplishments.

Unlike conventional tracking dashboards that merely display raw numbers, StackForge employs **Heuristic Purity Filtering** to automatically identify and quarantine unobtainable trophies (those tied to discontinued servers, removed content, or patched exploits). This results in a **True Completion Ratio (TCR)**—a metric that reflects only what you can genuinely achieve, giving you a fair standing against the global leaderboard.

The platform is built for the discerning collector who values **accuracy over vanity** and **longevity over instant gratification**. Whether you're a bronze-tier enthusiast or a platinum obsessive, StackForge adapts to your hunting style through adaptive difficulty weighting and dynamic percentile rankings.

## Feature Vault 🗝️

### 🔄 Stack Fusion Engine
The heart of StackForge. This proprietary algorithm examines game IDs, title metadata, and trophy structure similarity to determine safe merge candidates. You retain full control—approve, reject, or manually reconfigure each proposed fusion. The engine learns from your preferences over time, improving its suggestions with every interaction.

### 🧹 Unobtainable Trophy Filter
Automatic detection of trophies that are:
- Tied to decommissioned multiplayer servers
- Dependent on expired seasonal events
- Linked to delisted downloadable content
- Exploit-patched or otherwise irrelevant

These are moved to a **Shadow Archive**, visible only to you, so your public profile reflects achievable excellence.

A weighted percentage that accounts for difficulty, playtime investment, and rarity. A 100% TCR on a brutal action-RPG outweighs a 100% on a visual novel—our system respects your effort.

### 🌐 Multilingual Achievement Replication
Trophy names and descriptions auto-translate into 12 major languages, allowing you to read the lore behind each achievement without switching console settings.

### 📱 Responsive Mobile Architecture
The interface fluidly adapts from a 4K desktop monitor to a pocket-sized smartphone screen. Gesture controls allow swipe-to-merge actions on touch devices.

### 🛠️ Custom Challenge Builder
Create personal milestones (e.g., "Complete 100 hard-mode trophies this quarter") and track progress with visual progress rings and milestone confetti.

### 🤖 Smart Notifications
Set alerts for when a trophy in your backlog becomes unobtainable due to server shutdowns—we monitor announcement feeds 24/7 so you're never caught off guard.

### 🧩 Import/Export Portability
